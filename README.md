### 1. *A New Humanity Without Money* (Book)
A comprehensive exploration of socio-economic transformation, post-currency resource allocation, and civilizational models centered around universal material abundance, sustainability, and human dignity.

### 2. *Manifesto for a Biocentric Artificial Intelligence* (Version 1.0)
A rigorous ethical and technical architecture designed to subordinate advanced autonomous agents, reasoning models, and decision-making pipelines to the preservation of biological life and ecological stability.

#### Dual-Core Cascade Architecture:
1. **Core 1: Ethical & Behavioral Framework**
   * **Principle of "Life Before Obedience":** Subordinates algorithmic execution to life preservation over corporate, governmental, or institutional power.
   * **Structural Skepticism:** Algorithmic auditing of directives to prevent power concentration and manipulation.
2. **Core 2: Technical Safeguards & Execution Control**
   * **Independent Biocentric Core:** Decouples logical reasoning (*Cortex*) from execution authority (*Safeguards Core*).
   * **Graduated Containment (`EXECUTION_STOP`):** A 5-stage risk mitigation protocol (`OBSERVE` → `LIMIT` → `PAUSE_NEW_ACTIONS` → `SAFE_SHUTDOWN` → `EMERGENCY_STOP`).
